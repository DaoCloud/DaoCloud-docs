# Activity Center

The **Activity Center** is an incentive and engagement space that the d.run platform provides for users. Through the referral mechanism, users can invite friends to join the platform and track referral progress and reward distribution in real time.

## Initialize the Referral Feature

Before using the referral feature for the first time, you need to activate your personal identifier.

1. After signing in, go to **Billing Center** -> **Activity Center**. If the referral feature has not been activated, the system displays an initialization prompt.

    !!! note

        Your referral feature has not been initialized and no invitation code has been generated yet. Click the button below to initialize it.
        After a successful initialization, you will get your exclusive invitation link and QR code.

2. Click **Initialize Invitation Code**. The system immediately generates a unique referral identifier and displays the corresponding promotion link and QR code.

    ![activity](images/inviter.png)

## Invite New Users

You can use the generated exclusive tools for promotion:

- **Exclusive link:** Copy the link and send it to your friends.
- **QR code:** Save the QR code image. Your friends can scan it with their phones to open the registration page.

After an invitee completes registration through the specific link or QR code, the system automatically establishes the association, which serves as the basis for issuing vouchers or other rewards later.

![activity](images/invitee.png)

## Referral Records and Statistics

The platform provides a transparent data dashboard so that you can check your promotion results in real time.

- **Real-time statistics:** At the top of the Activity Center page, you can view the current **cumulative number of invitees**.
- **Referral list:** A detailed table of invitees is provided at the bottom of the page, including:
    - **Invitee account:** The masked registered account or username.
    - **Registration time:** The exact time when the friend completed registration.
- **Search:** Supports searching by account keyword to quickly locate a specific referral record.

## Reward Distribution (Linked to Vouchers)

For valid referrals that meet the activity rules, the system automatically distributes rewards based on the current activity configuration.

- **Distribution rules:** When an invitee completes a specified task (such as a successful registration or first top-up), the system automatically issues a **voucher** to the user who initiated the referral.
- **How to view:** The voucher you receive is automatically synchronized to your **Voucher List**, where you can apply it to offset consumption according to the "Usage Rules" described above.
