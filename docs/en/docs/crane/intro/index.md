# What Is Cockpit

Cockpit is the operations visualization entry point in the Token Factory console. It aggregates compute capacity, token output, revenue and cost, and production health on a single screen, helping you keep track of the platform's business and operating status.

After you enter Cockpit, the [Business Overview Panel](../user-guide/singlepage.md) opens by default.

![Business Overview Panel](../images/singlepage.png)

## How to Access

1. Log in to the Token Factory console.
2. In the product navigation, click **Cockpit**.
3. You land on **Business Overview** by default. You can also switch to other enabled pages from the product submenu.

## What You Can Do

- **Business review (operations mode)**: view revenue, cost, gross profit, capacity monetization, and the customer consumption structure.
- **Organizational governance (enterprise mode)**: view department quota consumption, organization usage distribution, and usage trends.

## Differences Between Operations Mode and Enterprise Mode

Depending on the deployment mode, the metrics on the Business Overview Panel differ:

| Mode | Use case | What you see on the page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Operations mode** | External MaaS operations | Business revenue, capacity monetization funnel, customer consumption distribution, SLA, and more |
| **Enterprise mode** | Internal enterprise AI operations | Business overview for the current period, department quotas, organization usage distribution, and more |
